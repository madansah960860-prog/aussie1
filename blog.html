What gets written about here

Three subjects, mostly. Technique, where explaining why something works is more useful than another recipe. Shopping, because most of the difference between good and mediocre home cooking happens before you get to the kitchen. And equipment, where the honest answer is usually that you do not need to buy anything.

Articles are updated when we learn something that changes the advice, and the update date is shown at the top of the piece. We do not quietly rewrite old posts to chase search traffic.
