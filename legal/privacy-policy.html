Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, store, secure and disclose personal information, and what you can do about it. Written to comply with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles.

Last updated 15/08/2026. Version 1.0.

Please note. This policy is a starting template and must be reviewed by an Australian-qualified lawyer before this site goes live. Do not rely on it as legal advice.

1. Who we are

This site is published by Sunlit Pantry Media Pty Ltd, ABN 12 345 678 901, of Level 2, 118 Gertrude Street, Fitzroy VIC 3065, Australia. In this policy, we, us and our mean Sunlit Pantry Media Pty Ltd.

You can contact us about privacy at privacy@sunlitpantry.com.au, by post at the address above, or by phone on +61 3 9000 0000.

We are bound by the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) contained in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth). This policy explains how we handle personal information, which the Act defines as information or an opinion about an identified individual, or an individual who is reasonably identifiable.

2. What personal information we collect

We collect only what we need to run the site and provide what you have asked for.

Information you give us directly

  • Contact form. Your name, email address, chosen topic and the content of your message.
  • Newsletter. Your email address, your first name if you give it, your chosen send frequency, and a record of the date, time and page at which you gave consent. We keep the consent record because the Spam Act 2003 requires us to be able to demonstrate consent.
  • Purchases. If you buy a cookbook, a meal-plan bundle or a membership, we collect your name, email address and billing details. Card details are handled by our payment processor and are never stored on our servers.
  • Correspondence. Anything you choose to tell us by email, post or telephone.

Information collected automatically

  • Server logs. Your IP address, browser type and version, device type, operating system, the page you requested, the referring page and the date and time of the request. These are collected by our hosting provider for security and troubleshooting.
  • Analytics. If you consent to analytics cookies, aggregated information about which pages are visited, how long people stay and how they arrived.
  • Advertising. If you consent to advertising cookies, our advertising partners including Google may collect information about your browsing in order to select and measure advertisements.

Information stored on your own device

Some features store information in your browser rather than sending it to us. Ticked ingredient checkboxes exist only while the page is open. Saved recipes and your cookie preference are stored in your browser's local storage on your device. We cannot read this information, and clearing your browser data removes it.

Sensitive information

We do not seek and do not want sensitive information as defined in the Privacy Act, which includes health information, racial or ethnic origin, political opinions, religious beliefs and sexual orientation. Please do not send it to us. If you tell us about a food allergy in a message, we will use it only to answer your question and will delete it with the rest of the correspondence.

3. Why we collect it, and how we use it

We use personal information only for the purpose it was collected for, for a directly related purpose you would reasonably expect, or where you have consented. Specifically, to:

  • reply to your enquiry, correction or recipe request;
  • send the newsletter you asked for, and manage your subscription preferences;
  • supply and support any product or membership you have purchased, and issue receipts;
  • operate, secure and improve the site, including diagnosing faults and preventing abuse;
  • understand in aggregate which recipes people use, so we know what to make next;
  • display advertising, where you have consented to advertising cookies;
  • meet our legal obligations, including record keeping under the Spam Act 2003 and tax law.

We do not use your personal information to make automated decisions that have a legal or similarly significant effect on you.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

A cookie is a small text file placed on your device by a website. We use three categories.

  • Essential cookies are needed for the site to work, including remembering your cookie choice. These are always on and do not require consent.
  • Analytics cookies help us understand how the site is used, in aggregate.
  • Advertising cookies are set by third parties including Google to select and measure advertising.

No non-essential cookie is set before you choose. When you first visit, a banner offers Accept all, Reject non-essential and Manage preferences. You can change your mind at any time using the Cookie settings link in the footer of every page.

Google and advertising cookies

Where you consent to advertising cookies, third party vendors including Google may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and other sites on the internet. You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings, and you can opt out of some third party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising through the industry opt out pages operated by the Digital Advertising Alliance and the Network Advertising Initiative.

Full detail, including a list of the cookies we set, is in our Cookie Policy.

5. Who we disclose personal information to

We do not sell personal information. We do not rent or trade mailing lists. We disclose personal information only to the following categories of recipient, and only as far as necessary:

  • Hosting and infrastructure providers, who store the site and its logs.
  • Email service providers, who send the newsletter and store the subscriber list.
  • Payment processors, who handle purchases. They receive your billing details directly.
  • Analytics providers, where you have consented to analytics cookies.
  • Advertising partners, including Google, where you have consented to advertising cookies.
  • Professional advisers such as accountants and lawyers, where necessary.
  • Law enforcement or regulators, where we are required or authorised by law.

Sponsors and advertisers never receive your email address or any other contact detail from us.

6. Overseas disclosure

Some of the service providers described above store or process data outside Australia. Depending on the provider, this may include the United States, Ireland, Singapore and other countries where those providers operate data centres.

Before disclosing personal information to an overseas recipient, we take reasonable steps under APP 8 to ensure the recipient does not breach the Australian Privacy Principles, generally by using providers with contractual data protection commitments. You should be aware that overseas recipients may be subject to foreign laws, and that in some cases you may not be able to seek redress under the Privacy Act if an overseas recipient mishandles your information.

7. How we store and secure personal information

Personal information is held on servers operated by our hosting and service providers. We take reasonable steps to protect it from misuse, interference, loss and unauthorised access, modification or disclosure, including:

  • serving the entire site over HTTPS;
  • restricting access to personal information to people who need it to do their job;
  • using multi-factor authentication on administrative accounts;
  • keeping software and dependencies patched;
  • collecting as little personal information as possible in the first place.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Notifiable Data Breaches scheme

We are covered by the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme under Part IIIC of the Privacy Act 1988. If we become aware of a data breach that is likely to result in serious harm to any individual whose personal information is involved, and we are not able to remediate it in time to prevent that harm, we will notify the affected individuals and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner as soon as practicable, as the scheme requires.

9. Accessing and correcting your information

Under APP 12 and APP 13 you may request access to the personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct it if it is inaccurate, out of date, incomplete, irrelevant or misleading.

To make a request, email privacy@sunlitpantry.com.au or write to us at the postal address above. Please tell us what information you are after so we can find it. We may need to verify your identity before releasing anything, which usually just means replying from the email address we hold.

We will respond within 30 days. Access is free. If a request is complex or repeated we may charge a reasonable cost-based fee, and we will tell you what it is before doing the work. If we refuse access or refuse to correct information, we will explain why in writing and tell you how to complain.

You may also ask us to delete your information. We will do so unless we are required by law to keep it, for example tax records or consent records under the Spam Act.

10. Unsubscribing from email

Every marketing email we send contains a working unsubscribe link, as required by the Spam Act 2003. One click removes you, at no cost, and takes effect immediately and in any case within five working days. You can also reply to any email and ask us to remove you, or email privacy@sunlitpantry.com.au.

11. Anonymity and pseudonymity

Under APP 2 you may deal with us anonymously or under a pseudonym where it is lawful and practicable. You can read every recipe on this site without telling us anything about yourself. We only need identifying information when you ask for a reply, subscribe to the newsletter, or buy something.

12. Children

This site is intended for a general adult audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 15. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Third party links

The site links to other websites, including retailers through affiliate links. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites. Their policies apply once you leave ours, and we suggest you read them.

14. How to complain

If you think we have breached the Australian Privacy Principles or mishandled your personal information, please tell us first. Email privacy@sunlitpantry.com.au with the word Complaint in the subject line, or write to the postal address above, and describe what happened.

We will acknowledge your complaint within 5 business days and give you a written response within 30 days. If we need longer, we will tell you why and agree a new timeframe with you.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC), which is the independent regulator for privacy in Australia. The OAIC can be contacted on 1300 363 992, by post at GPO Box 5218, Sydney NSW 2001, or through the complaints form on its website at oaic.gov.au. The OAIC generally expects you to raise the matter with us first.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version and its date are always shown at the top of this page. Where a change is significant, we will say so on the site, and where the change affects how we use information you have already given us, we will notify subscribers by email.
