August in Australia, late winter

Cook what is cheap and good right now

Southern Hemisphere seasons run the other way to most recipe sites. Our produce guide is written for Australian growing seasons, so summer means December to February and August means brassicas, citrus and root vegetables at their best value.

This month the greengrocer is full of cauliflower, leek, silverbeet, mandarins and blood oranges. Broccoli and cabbage are cheap. Tomatoes are not, so recipes leaning on tinned tomatoes will taste better and cost less.

How this site is funded

Every recipe on Sunlit Pantry is free to read, and there is no paywall on any cooking content. Running a recipe site costs money, so here is exactly where ours comes from, in plain English.

  1. Display advertising

    We sell advertising space on the site through advertising partners including Google. Ads are kept below the main content, are labelled as advertising, and never sit on top of a recipe or a button.

  2. Affiliate commissions

    Some links to kitchen equipment and pantry ingredients earn us a small commission if you buy. The disclosure sits next to the link itself, not just in the footer, and the price you pay is unchanged.

  3. Paid cookbooks and meal plan bundles

    We sell downloadable PDF cookbooks and seasonal meal-plan bundles, priced in Australian dollars. Buying one is optional and never unlocks recipes that were previously free.

  4. Optional membership

    Pantry Membership is $6.50 AUD per month for ad-free browsing, printable recipe cards and saved collections.

  5. Sponsored recipes and newsletter sponsorship

    We occasionally develop a recipe paid for by a brand. Those pages carry a sponsorship label at the very top, before the recipe. Newsletter sponsorship is labelled the same way inside the email.
