What is actually in it

  • The two or three recipes published that week, with a note on why we made them.

How we handle your consent and your details

Australian marketing email is governed by the Spam Act 2003, which requires three things of any commercial electronic message. We meet all three, and we would anyway.

  1. Express consent

    You tick the consent box on this page and then confirm by clicking a link in an email we send you. We do not add people from contact forms, competitions or purchased lists, and we do not rely on inferred consent.

  2. Clear sender identity

    Every email states that it is from Sunlit Pantry Media Pty Ltd, includes our ABN and Australian postal address, and gives a reply address that a person reads.

  3. A working unsubscribe

    One click, in every email, at no cost to you. It takes effect immediately and no later than five working days, and we do not ask you to log in or fill in a survey first. Unsubscribing removes you from the list entirely.

What we collect and why

Your email address, because we cannot send you an email without it. Your first name if you give it, so the email can address you properly. Your chosen frequency. We also record the date, time and page on which you gave consent, because the Spam Act requires us to be able to demonstrate that consent was given.

Our email service provider stores this data and may process it outside Australia. That, and your rights to access, correct or delete your information under the Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles, is set out in the privacy policy.

We do not sell or rent the list. Advertisers who sponsor the newsletter never receive your address. They pay for a labelled placement inside the email and nothing else.

Not ready to sign up?

That is completely fine. Everything in the newsletter is published on the site as well, and nothing on this site is held back for subscribers.
